## Phase 2: Partner Program Videos

### Video 3: Partner Program Overview
**Title:** "How Partners Make Money Referring Customers & Notaries"
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes
**Target:** Potential partners (consultants, agencies, influencers, business networks)

**Content Structure:**
1. **Hook (0:00-0:20)**
   - "Want to earn money by connecting people with notary services? Our partner program pays you for every successful conversion."

2. **How It Works (0:20-1:30)**
   - **Step 1: Sign up as a partner**
     - Quick registration process
     - Get your unique referral links and codes
   - **Step 2: Share with your network**
     - Refer customers who need notary services
     - Refer notaries looking for clients
     - Share on social media, email, website, or in person
   - **Step 3: Earn on conversion**
     - Customer publishes an offer → you earn when they complete a transaction
     - Notary joins and gets booked → you earn a percentage of their earnings
     - Track everything in real-time from your dashboard

3. **Payment Details (1:30-2:30)**
   - **Commission structure:**
     - X% on customer transactions (specify percentage)
     - Y% on notary earnings (specify percentage)
     - Recurring commissions for the lifetime of the referred user
   - **Payment methods:**
     - Stripe Connect for automatic payouts
     - Direct bank wire transfer
     - Minimum payout threshold: $Z
     - Payment frequency: Weekly/Monthly
   - **Admin dashboard:**
     - See all referrals in real-time
     - Track conversions and earnings
     - Download invoices and tax documents
     - Manage payout preferences

4. **Why Partner With Us (2:30-3:00)**
   - High conversion rates (customers have immediate needs)
   - Transparent tracking - no black box
   - Reliable payments - no chasing invoices
   - Marketing materials provided
   - Dedicated partner support

5. **Success Stories (3:00-3:30)**
   - Example: "John, a business consultant, refers 10 clients per month and earns $X,XXX passively"
   - Example: "Jane, a real estate agency, refers both buyers and notaries, earning $X,XXX monthly"

6. **Call to Action (3:30-4:00)**
   - "Ready to start earning? Join the partner program today - approval takes 24 hours."
   - Show partner sign-up URL
   - Link to partner FAQ and support

**Production Notes:**
- Show actual partner dashboard
- Animate the referral flow
- Include screenshots of payment confirmations (blurred sensitive data)
- Testimonials from existing partners

## Content Production Guidelines

### Video Production
- **Quality:** 1080p minimum, 4K preferred
- **Audio:** Clear, professional microphone
- **Lighting:** Well-lit, professional look
- **Branding:** Consistent intro/outro, lower thirds, colors
- **Captions:** All videos must have accurate captions
- **Thumbnails:** Custom, eye-catching, consistent style
- **Length:** Keep it concise - only as long as needed

### Content Calendar
- Plan 1 month ahead
- Batch record when possible
- Leave room for timely content
- Coordinate across platforms
- Track performance and adjust

### Metrics to Track
- **YouTube:** Views, watch time, subscribers, engagement, click-through rate
- **LinkedIn:** Impressions, engagement, followers, profile views
- **Twitter:** Impressions, engagements, follower growth
- **Instagram:** Reach, engagement, follower growth, story completion
- **TikTok:** Views, likes, shares, follower growth
- **Email:** Open rate, click-through rate, unsubscribe rate

### Tools
- **Video editing:** Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve
- **Graphics:** Canva, Figma, or Adobe Creative Suite
- **Scheduling:** Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later
- **Analytics:** Native platform analytics + Google Analytics
- **Email:** Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or similar

## Budget Considerations

### Video Production
- **DIY:** $0 (time investment)
- **Freelancer:** $500-2,000 per video
- **Agency:** $2,000-10,000 per video

### Other Costs
- **Equipment:** $500-2,000 (camera, microphone, lighting)
- **Software:** $50-200/month (editing, scheduling, analytics)
- **Stock footage:** $50-200/month (if needed)
- **Paid promotion:** $500-5,000/month (optional)

### Recommended Approach
Start with DIY for first 10 videos to validate content, then invest in professional production for key videos (investor pitch, main onboarding).
