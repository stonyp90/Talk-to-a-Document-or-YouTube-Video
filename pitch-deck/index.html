Product

Three steps. About a minute.

01

Bring your source

A PDF contract. A YouTube tutorial. A research paper. Drop it in. Earthly ingests and indexes it instantly.

02

Start talking

Ask "What is the termination clause?" or swipe down to summarize. Use your voice, your hands, or your eyes.

03

Go deeper

Follow up in your own words. "Flag any risks." "Where does the auth flow start?" Earthly remembers context.

Works on web and mobile. No dedicated hardware. No keyboard required.

Technology

Built for the post-keyboard era

Real-time voice engine

WebRTC streaming to the OpenAI Realtime API. Sub-second latency. Interruptible. Full duplex. Your voice never leaves the encrypted channel.

WebRTC · E2E encrypted

Motion & gesture recognition

On-device camera tracks hand gestures and body motion. Swipe, point, pinch. Works without wearables or depth sensors.

On-device · No wearables

Gaze & attention tracking

Eye position drives the cursor. Dwell to select. The UI knows where you are looking and responds accordingly.

Spatial computing

Universal content parser

PDFs, YouTube captions, web pages, documents. One extraction pipeline. Content is indexed once, queried forever.
