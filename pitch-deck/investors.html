Technology

Real-time voice engine

WebRTC streaming to the OpenAI Realtime API. Sub-second latency. Interruptible. Full duplex. Your voice never leaves the encrypted channel.

Motion & gesture recognition

On-device camera tracks hand gestures and body motion. Swipe, point, pinch. Works without wearables or depth sensors.
